# Design

- **Game Designer**:  
Designs gameplay mechanics, rules, and systems.
- **Level Designer**:  
Builds and arranges game levels for fun and flow.
- **Narrative Designer / Writer**:  
Crafts story, dialogue, quests, and world lore.
- **UI/UX Designer**:  
Designs user interfaces and ensures smooth player experience.

# Art & Asset Creation

- **3D Artist**:  
Models characters, environments, and props in 3D.
- **Texture Artist**:  
Creates color, roughness, normal, and other maps for surfaces.
- **Material Artist / Technical Artist**:  
Builds materials and shaders in-engine; bridges art and tech.
- **Concept Artist**:  
Designs visual style, characters, and environments through 2D sketches.
- **Animator**:  
Brings characters and objects to life with movement and expressions.
- **Rigger**:  
Creates skeletons and controls for 3D models to enable animation.
- **VFX Artist**:  
Designs visual effects like fire, smoke, magic, and explosions.
- **SFX Artist (Sound Designer)**:  
Creates and implements sound effects.
- **Composer**:  
Writes music and score for the game.

# Programming

- **Gameplay Programmer**:  
Implements player controls, mechanics, and interactions.
- **Engine Programmer**:  
Works on core engine features like rendering or physics.
- **Graphics Programmer**:  
Optimizes rendering, lighting, and shaders.
- **AI Programmer**:  
Codes NPC behavior and decision-making.
- **Network Programmer**:  
Handles multiplayer, synchronization, and server logic.
- **Tools Programmer**:  
Builds internal tools for artists and designers.
- **Audio Engineer**:  
Integrates and optimizes audio in the engine.
